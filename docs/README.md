# GridPortfolio

## AI-Accelerated Energy Portfolio Integration

GridPortfolio is an open-source reference architecture demonstrating
how fragmented energy organizations can turn disconnected data,
analytics, asset-management, commercial, market, and procurement
signals into one trusted portfolio view.

The core idea is:

> **Specialist ownership + shared portfolio context.**

The project is designed around a problem increasingly important to
large energy portfolios:

> Energy organizations can have excellent specialists in Asset
> Management, Data & Analytics, Wholesale, Origination, Procurement,
> and Program Management while still lacking the connective tissue
> that turns those specialist outputs into coordinated portfolio
> decisions.

GridPortfolio demonstrates what that connective tissue could look
like.

---

# The Problem

Imagine six teams independently operating an energy portfolio.

### Asset Management sees:

> Load at a major data-center asset has accelerated.

### Data & Analytics sees:

> Forecast error and uncertainty have increased.

### Wholesale sees:

> Market volatility and exposure are increasing.

### Origination sees:

> A renewable contract expires in 74 days.

### Procurement sees:

> No replacement sourcing event has been initiated.

### Program Management sees:

> The procurement initiative is blocked by a forecast approval
> dependency.

Each team has useful information.

No individual team necessarily sees the complete picture.

GridPortfolio connects the information.

The system recognizes:
